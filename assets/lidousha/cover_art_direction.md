# Ordinary talk cover research projection

This asset applies to a future normal ordinary single-host talk cover when the
selected treatment is an AI story redraw. It is a deployable projection of the
completed cover study, not a claim that the study proved a winning style or
layout.

Public reference material (descriptive only):

- version: `public-cover-art-direction.v1`
- primary public examples: `https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV13Sac66Eji`,

- applies to: future ordinary single-host talk covers; the current story and
  source material choose the visual language and layout
- limits: descriptive evidence only; no causal CTR evidence, no fixed style or
  layout ranking, and no automatic stranger-comprehension claim

The director must first state in natural language who encounters what, how the
host responds, and whether the wording is an immediate event, a past retelling,
a game or work fiction, or a hypothetical/modal statement. Use that statement
to decide what the picture and the short local text each carry. When a concrete
response is the hook, one host's accurate pose and words can be sufficient; do
not add a second person or object merely to make the frame busier. When a
target, object, or before/after change is the actual hook, a supported relation
or sequence may make that fact visible. Keep the real subject, number,
condition, strength, and modality.

Choose layout and visual language from the story, the source identity frame,
and the readable text area. A previous successful recipe used a pale-cyan
paper-like field, rough, confident deep-navy contour lines, visible brush
texture, compact big-headed comic proportions, rounded features, and
restrained flat shading. Those are optional candidate cues, not a permanent
profile style or a QC gate; the phrase `paper illustration` describes that
optional candidate only, not every ordinary redraw.
The older lightning/thunder example is not a default event and must never be
copied into another story. Pink/yellow lettering is one possible contrast
choice, not a required palette. The trusted local text layer must record the
actual selected palette and use a clear verified font; the image generator must
draw no letters, labels, speech bubbles, logos, or UI.

Do not treat a pretty face, sweat drop, lightning, background, empty screen, or
generic surprised pose as evidence that the story was pictured. Do not invent a
participant, outfit, relationship, event, prop, current-time action, private
chat screenshot, or game character. A visual metaphor must remain visibly an
editorial metaphor. Preserve source-visible identity, outfit, accessories,
HOST_ONLY constraints, source bbox/crop and all existing provenance contracts.

An independently selected style sample is optional. If used, send it as a
`STYLE_ONLY` reference and bind its actual hash; it may inform only the visual
language selected for this story. Never copy its lightning, props, lettering,
costume, pose, identity, or story. Songs, game camera-window compositions,
verified relationship frames, and explicit screenshot or screenshot-polish
treatments keep their own contracts and do not inherit this asset.
